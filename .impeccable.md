## Design context

The full design is in `DESIGN.md`. This file is the short version for design tools.

### Users
Undergraduates aged 19 to 24 at a multi-faculty technical university, playing for their faculty. They open the Android app for a few seconds between classes, on the stairs, or at home in the evening. Jobs: make sure climbs count, see who is winning their building, pick or switch a building, compare on the season tables.

### Brand personality
Rowdy, concrete, scoreboard. A match day between faculties played in stairwells: loud team colours, confident numbers, short plain English. Never shames elevator use, never rewards speed.

### Aesthetic direction
Dark by decision: buildings with windows lit in team colours against a night sky. Night neutrals tinted to hue 262 and chalk text; the only accent is the player's own team colour, passed through the team tone rule in `DESIGN.md` section 5. Type: Big Shoulders Display (titles, codes), Big Shoulders Stencil Display (one hero numeral per screen), Barlow (text and tabular numbers). Not a fitness tracker, not a dashboard, no neon, glow or purple and cyan gradients.

### Design principles
1. The game comes first: your building and who holds it, before any status detail.
2. One primary action per screen; everything else is a text button or a row.
3. Colour belongs to the teams; chrome stays night and chalk.
4. Status speaks only when the player must act.
5. Numbers are exact and labelled; floors are always "floors (estimate)".
